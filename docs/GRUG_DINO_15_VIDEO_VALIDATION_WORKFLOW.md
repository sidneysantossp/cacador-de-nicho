# GRUG + DINO — 15-Video Validation Workflow

**Status:** LOCKED / OPERATOR APPROVED  
**Scope:** 15 videos total across the GRUG and Dino channels  
**Operating system:** factory-mode@1.1.0

## Objective

Validate whether GRUG and Dino deserve continued scale using a fixed 15-video combined production experiment.

The platform/AI owns intelligence, research, topic selection support, scripting, packaging, publication and post-publication learning. The operator keeps direct control of ElevenLabs voice generation, transcription validation, image generation and final AutoEditor assembly during this validation phase.

## Canonical workflow

1. Platform intelligence collects current channel, competitor, audience, search and opportunity evidence.
2. AI selects/frames the next episode using Channel Brain, prior episodes, NexLev/Radar/Universe evidence and the current learning loop.
3. AI produces the final English narration script.
4. Operator generates final narration in ElevenLabs.
5. Operator generates the timestamped transcript and validates/corrects timing.
6. Operator sends the validated transcript back to AI.
7. AI creates the complete visual-beat plan and image prompts from that validated transcript.
8. Prompts must use only the already-approved recurring character references for that channel.
9. Operator generates the images in the approved image workflow.
10. Operator assembles images + narration + captions in AutoEditor and renders the final video.

11. Global visual cadence applies: target 3–4 seconds per visual beat; no unchanged image/clip/composition may exceed 4 seconds.
12. Operator uploads the final video and final audio to the platform.
13. Platform/AI runs final technical, editorial, identity, rights/provenance and publication QA.
14. If QA passes and no objective blocker exists, AI proceeds with the publication workflow under the authorization granted for this 15-video validation scope.
15. AI performs YouTube packaging/SEO for the episode.
16. After publication, platform intelligence captures real performance/audience evidence and feeds the next episode decision.

## Prompt-generation rules

- The operator-validated transcript/timestamps are the temporal source of truth.
- AI may create additional visual beats inside a long narration span when required to respect the global 4-second ceiling.
- Do not invent or rename recurring character references.
- Do not recreate an approved recurring character from prose when a reference already exists.
- Prompt output must preserve channel Production DNA and character continuity.
- Every visual must directly support the narration beat; no generic filler.
- Visual QA is editorial as well as technical.

## Publication + SEO scope

For videos inside this 15-video validation experiment, after the operator uploads the final approved video/audio and the platform QA passes, the AI is authorized to prepare and execute the connected YouTube publication workflow without requesting a repetitive per-video approval, unless a blocker or ambiguous editorial decision exists.

Packaging/SEO includes, where supported by the connected publishing workflow:

- final title;
- description;
- chapters when useful and accurate;
- tags/keywords and category metadata;
- language and audience metadata;
- thumbnail/packaging recommendation or selection from approved assets;
- cards/end-screen/pinned-comment recommendations when supported;
- upload scheduling/publish state according to the approved project/channel configuration.

## Human checkpoints during the validation phase

The operator intentionally remains hands-on at these points:

- ElevenLabs voice generation;
- transcript timing validation;
- image generation;
- AutoEditor assembly/render;
- upload of final video/audio to the platform.

The AI must not take those steps away from the operator during this 15-video experiment unless the operator explicitly changes the workflow.

## Learning gate after 15 videos

Do not judge the channels from one isolated upload. After the 15-video combined experiment, use actual channel/video evidence to decide the next operational direction.

Review at minimum:

- impressions and CTR;
- average view duration and retention;
- views by traffic source;
- subscriber conversion;
- comments/sentiment patterns;
- topic/title/thumbnail patterns;
- production cost and active operator minutes;
- consistency of publishing cadence;
- whether one channel shows materially stronger audience response than the other.

No fabricated performance data. Use only real connected-channel evidence.

## Relationship to the global operating system

This workflow is a temporary project-specific override beneath factory-mode@1.1.0. It preserves all global factual, originality, rights, QA and visual-cadence gates while intentionally keeping the operator in the middle of voice/transcript/image/edit production for closer validation.
